Lien Writer® Pro

The top of the line. Everything Multi-State does, plus the tooling you need if producing lien paperwork for other companies is your business rather than a side effect of it.

What it includes

Full document coverage, everywhere we cover:

  • Preliminary notices, notices of furnishing, and the related pre-claim notices
  • Lien waivers and releases — conditional and unconditional, partial and final
  • Mechanics lien forms, stop notice forms, and mechanics lien release forms
  • All 50 US states, the Canadian provinces and territories, and the US territories
  • Public, private, and federal works

Plus the Pro tooling:

  • Tracking of every form printed, per client
  • Dynamic pricing schemas, so different clients can carry different rates
  • Invoicing for documents produced on behalf of clients — monthly billing that is easy to run and easy to reconcile
  • Rapid intake of job information submitted from a website, so you are not retyping what a client already typed

Who it’s for

Lien service bureaus, and any firm that produces notices, waivers, and liens as a service to companies in the construction industry.

If you are processing paperwork under your own name only, you almost certainly want Multi-State instead — it has the same document coverage without the billing machinery. Pro earns its keep the moment you need to invoice someone else for the documents you produced.

It is also the version to look at if you file under many company names, since the unlimited-companies capability carries over from Multi-State.

Honest scope

Pro is the widest licence, with the service-bureau features switched on. It produces documents; it does not file them for you, and it does not decide for you whether a lien is the right move on a given job. The forms are kept current with the help of attorneys in the jurisdictions we cover, but timing, service, and strategy remain your calls.

Pricing depends on version and company size, so it is not listed here. Ask and we will quote it.

What is live today

Lien Writer runs in your browser — there is nothing to install and nothing to upgrade. The document set is rolling out state by state, so ask us which documents are live for the states you work in and we will answer straight rather than sell you a promise.

Not sure this is the right version?

Tell us how many states you work in and how many companies you file under, and we will tell you plainly which version fits — no upselling.
