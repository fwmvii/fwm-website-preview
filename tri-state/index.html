Lien Writer® Tri-State

For companies working across a border or two. All the document coverage of Single-State, for any three jurisdictions you choose.

What it includes

  • Preliminary notices, notices of furnishing, and the related pre-claim notices
  • Lien waivers and releases — conditional and unconditional, partial and final
  • Mechanics lien forms, stop notice forms, and mechanics lien release forms
  • Any three states, Canadian provinces or territories, or US territories — you pick them
  • Public, private, and federal works in your chosen three

Who it’s for

Regional contractors and suppliers with a home state and a couple of neighbours. If your work maps onto a stable set of two or three jurisdictions, this is the version that stops you paying for forty-seven states you will never print.

Above three jurisdictions, Multi-State is the answer. Filing under more than one company name, even within your three states? That also means Multi-State — the additional-companies capability is exclusive to it.

Honest scope

Three jurisdictions means three, chosen at purchase. Picking up work in a fourth state is a conversation with us, not a setting you change on a Friday afternoon — so if a fourth is plausible within the year, price Multi-State before you decide.

Pricing depends on version and company size, so it is not listed here. Ask and we will quote it.

Desktop and web

These version names describe how the desktop Lien Writer program is licensed, and that is what this page sets out. The Lien Writer web application is rolling out the same document set tier by tier — ask us which documents are available in the web app today for the states you work in, and we will answer straight.
