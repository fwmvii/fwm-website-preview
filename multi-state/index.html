Lien Writer® Multi-State

For companies working everywhere. Every document, every jurisdiction we cover, and the ability to file under as many company names as you need.

What it includes

  • Preliminary notices, notices of furnishing, and the related pre-claim notices
  • Lien waivers and releases — conditional and unconditional, partial and final
  • Mechanics lien forms, stop notice forms, and mechanics lien release forms
  • All 50 US states, the Canadian provinces and territories, and the US territories
  • Public, private, and federal works
  • Unlimited additional companies — produce forms in the name of a DBA or a sister company

Who it’s for

Companies whose jobs cross state lines, and credit departments that would rather not think about which states their licence covers before saying yes to a project.

It is also the answer for a company that works in one state but files under more than one entity: only the Multi-State versions can produce forms for additional companies, so a single-state business with a DBA or a sister company often needs Multi-State anyway.

Working in three states or fewer, under one name? Tri-State will be cheaper. Running a large networked or thin-client environment? Ask us about the Citrix / Terminal Server edition.

Honest scope

Multi-State is a licence breadth, not a different program — the forms, the Tracker, the certified-mail numbering, and the printing behave the same as they do in Single-State. What changes is how many jurisdictions and how many companies you may produce documents for.

On Canadian and US-territory coverage: ask us which jurisdictions you specifically need before you buy. We would rather confirm the list against the forms we actually hold than have you find out afterwards.

Pricing depends on version and company size, so it is not listed here. Ask and we will quote it.
