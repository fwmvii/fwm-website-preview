Find out who actually owns the job site

A preliminary notice sent to the wrong owner protects nothing. Our Property Research service returns the ownership and parcel details for a job site so the notice you serve names the right party at the right address. You do not have to be a Lien Writer customer to order one.

What a report contains

Reports generally include:

  • The property owner’s name and mailing address
  • Information on the property itself, including the Assessor’s Parcel Number (APN)

Results vary with what the county and public records actually hold for a given parcel. We tell you what we found; we do not pad a report to fill a template.

Who it’s for

Anyone who needs to serve a notice or record a claim against a property and does not yet know, in writing, who owns it.

  • Subcontractors and suppliers preparing preliminary notices
  • Credit and AR teams verifying an owner of record before a deadline
  • Anyone who needs the APN for a lien or a stop notice

You do not need to be a Lien Writer customer. New to us? Order a report and we will set you up.
