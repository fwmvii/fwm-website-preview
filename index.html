Five versions, one platform

Every version produces the same documents to the same standard. What changes is how many states and how many companies you need to file for. Pricing depends on version and company size, so it isn’t listed here — ask and we’ll quote it.

Lite

One state, lighter workload.

Preliminary notices, notices of furnishing, waivers and releases, and mailing labels for a single state. The least expensive way in, and the right fit if your attorney prepares the liens themselves.

About Lite

Single-State

One state, every document.

Everything in Lite plus the full claim set — stop notices and lien claims — for the one state where you do business.

About Single-State

Tri-State

Up to three states.

The full document set across three states of your choosing — for companies whose work crosses a border or two but not the whole map.

About Tri-State

Multi-State

Everywhere, and every entity.

All 50 states, the Canadian provinces and territories, and the US territories — plus unlimited additional companies, so DBAs and sister companies file under their own names.

About Multi-State

Pro

For lien-services professionals.
