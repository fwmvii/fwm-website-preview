Lien Writer® Lite

The simplest version. Preliminary notices and waivers and releases for one jurisdiction — and deliberately not the lien forms themselves.

What it includes

  • Preliminary notices, notices of furnishing, and the related pre-claim notices
  • Lien waivers and releases — conditional and unconditional, partial and final
  • Any one state, Canadian province or territory, or US territory — you pick it
  • Public, private, and federal works in your chosen jurisdiction

What it does not include: mechanics lien forms, stop notice forms, and mechanics lien release forms. That is the difference between Lite and Single-State, and it is the only difference.

Who it’s for

Offices with a lighter workload, and companies whose attorneys prepare and record the liens. If a lien in your business always goes to counsel anyway, you are paying for forms you will never print — Lite is the version that admits that.

It is also the least expensive way into the program, which makes it a reasonable place to start if you are protecting lien rights properly for the first time and want the notices under control before anything else.

If you do file your own liens, or expect to, go to Single-State instead. Adding the lien forms later means changing versions.

Honest scope

We would rather be blunt about this one: Lite cannot produce a mechanics lien, a stop notice, or a lien release form. That is by design, not an oversight, and it is the single most common reason a customer moves up a version. If there is any chance you will need to record a claim yourself, price Single-State now and save the migration.

What Lite does do, it does exactly as well as every other version — same forms, same certified-mail numbering, same Tracker.

Pricing depends on version and company size, so it is not listed here. Ask and we will quote it.
